How to read

Each point on the surface is one pair of fields. Fields run along both edges in order of shared foundations, with the clustering tree on the front and left. The low blue ground is pairs that already combined, darker where they combined more recently. Orange peaks are pairs that have not combined but that the graph ranks high. On Smooth the surface is blurred over about two neighbouring fields, so a hill means many high-ranked gaps among related fields, and heights are relative to the tallest hill that year. On Each pair every ranked pair is its own spike, taller for a higher rank under the selected score. The surface is symmetric: pair A and B appears twice. Hover or click snaps to the nearest ranked pair. A peak is a ranking, not a probability.
